Editing & Proofreading Transformation ✏️

See how rough student sentences are refined into scholarly, high-scoring prose.

❌ Original Student Draft (Fragmented & Informal)

"Students are having a hard time focusing online because there is too much noise and distractions at their home. Also, the wifi disconnects a lot which causes them to fail tests and they feel very sad and stressed out about it."

Issues: Colloquial phrasing, run-on structure, lack of academic tone, subjective emotional descriptors.
✨ Soleil Comms Polished Submission (Academic & Rigorous)

"Empirical evidence demonstrates that students participating in remote learning environments face significant cognitive overload, primarily attributed to unmitigated domestic ambient distractions and recurring broadband instability—both of which critically correlate with heightened academic anxiety and declining assessment performance."

Improvements: Scholarly terminology, clear causal progression, formal diction, and precise thematic alignment.
